Phase 4 — ISI MStat

25 Feb – early May · 10 weeks

ISI tests the same content at greater depth and asks you to write it: PSA is objective (maths + stats), PSB is subjective (proofs and derivations). What's new is rigour, proof-writing and harder problems, kept deliberately narrow. Same weekly hours.

If ST went badly and you feel the base isn't there, still do this phase: it is the best possible preparation for GATE ST 2028 as well.

Rules

  1. A resource is done when its problems are done. Watch at 1.5×, pause, solve, then move on.
  2. One error log from day one. Topic, why wrong, correct idea. It is your January and April revision.
  3. One subject per weeknight. Switch subjects by day, not by hour.
  4. Don't buy a test series. Official PYQs (ST, DA, MA, CS, CAT) plus a few free CAT mocks are enough.
  5. If a deadline slips by more than 4 days, cut breadth, not the spine. Drop in this order: ISI real-analysis depth → ST multivariate → ST non-parametric → DA AI/warehousing extras → CAT weekday VA drills. Never cut probability, estimation, testing or linear algebra.
  6. Mid-December checkpoint: if Week 10 (estimation) isn't done by 20 Dec, make ISI a 2028 target and put its hours into ST. Nothing is lost — Phase 4 material is the same.
  7. Sleep and one half-day fully off every week. Part-time plans fail through burnout, not through lack of hours.
