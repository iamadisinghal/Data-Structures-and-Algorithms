# Waypoint Standalone guide (no Claude needed)

This guide takes you through the complete job search with Waypoint, using only your browser.
Nothing to install, no account, no AI. Plan on about **45 minutes to set up**, then
**30 minutes a day**.

> In the app, open the **Standalone** tab at the top, then **Start Here**. It shows these same
> steps with your progress ticked off automatically.

---

## Setup (once)

### Step 1: Build your master profile (10 min) - *Profile & Resume*

Your master profile is the single source of truth. Every tailored resume, cover letter and
message is built from it, and Waypoint never adds anything that isn't in it.

1. **Import resume** tab: paste your resume text, or upload a `.pdf`, `.txt` or `.md` file.
   (Word file? Save it as PDF first, or copy and paste the text.)
2. Click **Parse resume** and review what was found: roles, dates, bullets, skills, education.
   Click **Use this as my profile** (or **Merge** to only fill empty fields).
3. **Basics & targets** tab: add your headline, LinkedIn URL, target job titles, locations, work
   modes, salary (current, expected and walk-away minimum), notice period and work authorization.
4. **Skills** tab: add skills from the 1,000+ suggestions. Two helpers:
   - *Found in your experience, not listed*: skills your bullets already prove. Click to add.
   - *Wanted by your saved jobs, missing here*: add only if you truly have them.
5. **Experience** tab: use the **bullet coach**. Each bullet is rated *strong / ok / weak*; hover
   the chip for tips. Aim for a strong action verb + what you did + a number. The wand button
   applies a quick fix and leaves a placeholder for the result.
6. Watch the **profile strength** ring. Get it to at least 70%.

**Switching careers?** In Basics & targets, turn on **Career switch mode** and pick your from/to
fields. Summaries, cover letters and match scores then adapt to your switch.

### Step 2: Set goals and permissions (2 min) - *Data & Settings*

- **Weekly goals:** applications, outreach messages, follow-ups and learning sessions. Start
  realistic, e.g. 8 / 12 / 5 / 3.
- **Minimum match** for the planner to suggest applying (default 60%).
- **Permissions:** choose whether research and drafting run automatically. Sending messages and
  submitting applications are locked: you always do those yourself.
- **Region and currency:** decides which job boards you see and how salaries display.

### Step 3: Define targets (15 min) - *Market & Targets*

1. **Target companies:** add 5-15 companies. Tier them **A** (dream), **B** (strong fit) and
   **C** (backup), and rate your interest and fit. The *Where to focus* map shows which ones to
   prioritize (top right).
2. **Company research:** for each tier-A company, open the research links (careers page, news,
   Glassdoor, interview questions, salaries, Crunchbase, Reddit...) and fill in the notes:
   mission, products, recent news, culture, interview process, salary signals, key people and
   pain points. These notes are woven into your cover letters and interview prep.
3. **Career switch** (optional): see your skill overlap with the target field, transferable
   strengths, gaps (click to add to your learning plan), bridge roles and a 12-week roadmap.

## The daily loop (about 30 minutes)

### 1. Approval Queue (5 min)

The planner turns your goals and pipeline into concrete proposals, for example:

- *Tailor resume for Cobalt Commerce (71% match)*
- *Find people at Lumen Health: no contacts at a tier-A company*
- *Message Sana Kapoor (alumni, warmth 3/5)*, with a draft you can edit on the card
- *Follow up on Lumen Health: applied 9 days ago*
- *Close a skill gap: Airflow (in 3 saved jobs)*

Each card shows the **reason**, **impact**, **effort** and **risk tier**. Click **Approve**,
**Dismiss**, or **Approve all safe** (Tier-1 only). Approved items move to **Ready to do**, where
**Do it now** opens a guided flow. Click **Run planner** any time for fresh proposals.

### 2. Discover and save jobs (10 min) - *Job Discovery*

- **Discover** tab: the search builder opens pre-filtered searches on every major board for your
  region, plus remote-first boards.
- **Hidden jobs:** one click searches company hiring systems (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday,
  SmartRecruiters...) directly. Postings often appear there first, with less competition.
- Found a good one? Click **Add job** and **paste the full job description**. You instantly get:
  - a **match score** with a breakdown (skills, must-haves, years, title, education, location)
  - must-have and nice-to-have skills, split into what you have and what you're missing
  - red flags ("competitive salary" with no range, 24/7 language...) and positive signals
  - the description with your skills highlighted green and gaps red

### 3. Tailor and apply (15 min per application) - *Apply Wizard*

Open any saved job and click **Apply Wizard**:

1. **Review fit:** go / no-go guidance and missing must-haves.
2. **Resume:** one click generates a tailored version. Fine-tune it in **Resume Studio**:
   - 3 ATS-safe templates, summary variants, bullets-per-role density
   - an **honesty check**: tick only the missing skills you genuinely have
   - click any bullet in the preview to edit it for this version
   - an **ATS score** with 12 checks (keywords, must-haves, metrics, length, weak phrases...)
   - export to **PDF** (print dialog: "Save as PDF"), **Word**, text, HTML or Markdown
3. **Cover letter:** 5 tones (professional, enthusiastic, concise, career changer, referral).
   Edit, save and export.
4. **Referral check:** shows your contacts at the company, warmest first. Ask for a referral
   before (or right after) applying.
5. **Answers & autofill:** drafts for common screening questions ("Why us?", years with X,
   salary, notice period). Copy them into the form.
6. **Submit & track:** open the posting, fill it in, click Submit **yourself**, then click
   **I submitted my application**. The job moves to Applied and a follow-up is set for 7 days.

**Autofill Kit (set up once):** fill in your answer bank, then drag the **Waypoint Autofill**
button to your bookmarks bar. On any application form, click it: standard fields (name, email,
phone, links, location, work authorization, notice period, salary...) fill in and turn green.
Test it on the built-in demo form first. It never clicks Submit.

### 4. Reach out (5-10 min) - *People & Network, Outreach*

- **Contacts:** add ex-colleagues, alumni, friends and mentors first; warm contacts reply far
  more often. Rate warmth from 1 to 5. **Import LinkedIn CSV** brings in your connections at
  target companies (LinkedIn: Settings > Data privacy > Get a copy of your data > Connections).
- **Find people:** for each target company, ready-made LinkedIn and Google searches for
  recruiters, hiring managers, peers, alumni of your schools and ex-colleagues from your past
  employers. Add the people you find.
- **Network map** and **Warm paths:** see your best route into each company and what to do next.
- **Outreach:** pick a person and a message type, add one personal detail, then:
  1. **Send now (guided):** copy, open LinkedIn or email, then click **I sent it**.
  2. **Queue for later**, or **Save draft**.

  Mark replies in the outreach log to keep your reply-rate chart accurate.

### 5. Track (2 min) - *Applications*

Drag cards across the kanban: Saved > Applying > Applied > Screening > Interview > Offer (or
Rejected/Withdrawn). Cards show match, age, next step, and whether a resume and letter are
ready. "Stale" flags applications with no news for 3+ weeks.

## Interviews and offers

- **Story bank** (Interview Prep): write 6-8 STAR stories. The coverage chart shows which
  competencies (leadership, conflict, failure, influence...) still need a story. You can start a
  story from any resume bullet.
- **Practice:** random behavioral and role-specific questions (26 role families) with a
  2-minute timer, a suggested story, and a self-rating log.
- **Company prep:** per job, a briefing from your research, likely questions mapped to your
  stories, a readiness checklist, questions to ask, and a thank-you note draft.
- **30-60-90 plan:** generated from the job description; edit and bring it to final rounds.
- **Offers:** add each offer (base, bonus, equity per year, joining bonus, benefits, growth,
  work-life balance). Compare total compensation, adjust what matters to you, and use the
  **negotiation helper** for a recommended ask plus email and phone scripts.

---

## Weekly review (15 min, Fridays)

1. **Command Center:** applications vs. goal, response rate, funnel conversion, consistency
   heatmap.
2. **Market insights:** which skills keep appearing? Add real gaps to your **learning plan**.
3. Research 2 new target companies. Re-tier if needed.
4. Practice 3 interview questions out loud.
5. **Data & Settings > Export backup.**

## FAQ and troubleshooting

**Where is my data?** In this browser's local storage, on this computer only. Clearing site data
erases it, so export backups. To move computers: Export backup, then Import file on the other one.

**Does Waypoint apply for me?** No, by design. It prepares everything; you press Submit. That
keeps applications accurate, honest and within job sites' terms.

**The PDF upload didn't work.** PDF reading loads a small library from the internet. If you're
offline or it's blocked, paste the text instead.

**The bookmarklet filled nothing.** Some sites use custom widgets or cross-site frames that
browsers protect. Fill those fields by hand. Re-install the bookmarklet after changing answers.

**Can I use it on my phone?** Yes. The layout adapts, though tailoring and applying are easier
on a larger screen.

**Match scores look off.** They're offline estimates based on skills in the job description.
Paste the full description, keep your skills list complete, and treat the score as a guide.
